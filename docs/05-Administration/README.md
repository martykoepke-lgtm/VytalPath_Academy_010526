# Office Administration

Standard operating procedures for daily office operations.

**CMAA Domain:** D7 - Medical Practice Administrative Procedures (16% of exam)

## Content Structure

- Phone System Login & Setup
- Cash Drawer Opening
- Pre-Scrubbing the Schedule

### Closing the Office
- Cash Drawer Closing & Reconciliation
- End-of-Day Reconciliation & Deposit

### Administrative Tasks
- No-Show Letters
- Ordering Front Office Supplies
- Monthly Compliance Logs

SOPs for this section are defined in `src/data/sopContent.ts` and rendered via the AdministrationSection component.
