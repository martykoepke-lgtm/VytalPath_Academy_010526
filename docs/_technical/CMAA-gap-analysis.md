   - CMS billing requirements
   - Medicare vs Medicaid
   - Claims, EOBs, ERAs
   - Collections and adjustments

   - Opening/closing procedures
   - Medical records management
   - Filing systems
   - Financial procedures (petty cash, reconciliation)
   - Computer skills assessment

   - Patient's Bill of Rights

6. **Prior Authorization & Referrals** (Domains 4, 5)
   - Referral process
   - Prior auth initiation
   - Tracking authorizations
   - Appeals basics

### Tier 3: Important Additions

   - ICD-10 structure

8. **Telehealth Support** (Domains 4, 5)
