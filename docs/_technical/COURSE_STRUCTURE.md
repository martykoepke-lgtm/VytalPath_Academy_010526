# VytalPath Academy - Course Structure

## Overview

VytalPath Academy offers structured learning paths for healthcare front office professionals. Courses are organized into modules, each containing video lessons, reading materials, and knowledge assessments.

**Time Estimates:**
- Videos: 4-5 minutes each
- Readings: 8-10 minutes each
- Quizzes: 5-10 minutes each

---

## Course 1: Healthcare Foundations

**Type:** Foundation Course (Required for all roles)
**Estimated Time:** ~2 hours
**Certificate:** Healthcare Foundations Certificate

**Purpose:** Build the foundation for any healthcare administrative role. Learn about healthcare settings, medical law and ethics, insurance basics, and essential terminology. This course is a prerequisite for role-specific courses.

### Module 1: Healthcare Settings
Understanding the different types of healthcare environments and your role within them.

## Course 2: Front Office Specialist

**Type:** Role Specialty Course
**Prerequisite:** Healthcare Foundations
**Estimated Time:** ~1 hour (expanding as modules are added)
**Certificate:** Front Office Specialist Certificate

**Purpose:** Master the daily operations of a healthcare front office. Deep-dive into insurance verification, patient scheduling, check-in/check-out procedures, and payment collection.

### Module 1: Insurance Operations
Master the day-to-day insurance tasks: verifying eligibility, understanding benefits, and collecting patient responsibility.

| Lesson | Type | Duration | Status |
|--------|------|----------|--------|
