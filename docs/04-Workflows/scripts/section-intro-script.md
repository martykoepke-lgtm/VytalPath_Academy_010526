# Navigating Workflows - Section Intro Script

**Video Type:** Section Introduction (Coach Mode)
**Duration:** 60-90 seconds
**Tone:** Warm, practical, reassuring

---

## Script

### [Acknowledge]
"Every clinic does things a little differently, right? So you might be wondering — how is this section going to help me when I don't even know what system my clinic uses?"

### [Reframe]
"Here's what I've found: while the specific software changes from place to place, the core workflows are remarkably similar. Check-in, check-out, scheduling, handling phone calls — the sequence of steps, the information you need to collect, the questions to ask. That stays the same."

### [Preview]
"This section gives you both the 'why' and the 'how.' Video lessons walk you through registration and scheduling workflows step by step. And then we've got something special — 24 quick-reference guides. These are the step-by-step checklists you can actually use on the job."

### [Navigate]
"See the toggle up here? 'Video Lessons' is where you learn the workflows in depth. 'Quick Reference' — that's your SOP library. Think of those as your cheat sheets. When you're at work and you need a refresher on patient check-out, you can pull up that guide and follow along. You can even bookmark your favorites."

### [Encourage]
"These workflows are going to become second nature. But until they do, you've got backup right here. Let's walk through them together."

---

## Production Notes

- **Setting:** Professional but approachable
- **Delivery:** Practical, matter-of-fact, reassuring
- **Acknowledge:** The valid concern about clinic-specific differences
- **Highlight:** The 24 SOPs are a unique, practical resource
- **UI demo:** Show the toggle between Video Lessons and Quick Reference

## Key Messages to Convey

1. Clinic differences are real, but core workflows are universal
2. Learn the principles, adapt to the specifics
3. Video lessons for understanding, SOPs for doing
4. 24 quick-reference guides = on-the-job support
5. These become second nature with practice
