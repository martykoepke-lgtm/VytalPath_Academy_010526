\[warm\] You'll start with opening the office. \[pause\] Logging into the phone system \[pause\] setting up the cash drawer \[pause\] pre-scrubbing the schedule so you know what's coming. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] Then before-the-visit workflows. \[pause\] Registering new patients \[pause\] scheduling existing patients \[pause\] and making reminder calls that actually prevent no-shows. \[pause\] Those are video lessons that walk you through each one step by step. \[pause\]

\[warm\] During the visit \[pause\] you'll learn check-in procedures for new patients, existing patients, and walk-ins. \[pause\] Plus checkout workflows \[pause\] scheduling follow-ups, collecting payments, and making sure nothing gets missed. \[pause\]
