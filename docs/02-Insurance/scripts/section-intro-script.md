# Insurance Training - Section Intro Script

**Video Type:** Section Introduction (Coach Mode)
**Duration:** 60-90 seconds
**Tone:** Warm, encouraging, demystifying

---

## Script

### [Acknowledge]
"Insurance. I know. Even people who've worked in healthcare for years still find insurance confusing sometimes. If you're feeling a little intimidated right now, you're definitely not alone."

### [Reframe]
"But here's what I want you to know: insurance isn't actually complicated — it's just that nobody ever explains it clearly. There's a logic to it. Once you see that logic, everything starts to click."

### [Preview]
"We're going to break it all down. You'll learn how insurance actually works, the different types of payers you'll see, and those terms — copays, deductibles, coinsurance — that get thrown around constantly. Then we'll get practical: reading insurance cards, verifying eligibility, collecting payments. The stuff you'll do every day."

### [Navigate]
"You've got two modules here. Start with Insurance Basics — that's your foundation. Then move to Insurance Operations — that's where it gets hands-on. After the lessons, you'll see there's an interactive exercise at the bottom. That's a drag-and-drop matching game to test yourself on the key terms. It's actually kind of fun."

### [Encourage]
"By the end of this section, when a patient asks 'What's my copay?' or 'Has my deductible been met?' — you'll know exactly how to help them. Let's demystify this together."

---

## Production Notes

- **Setting:** Professional but approachable
- **Delivery:** Empathetic acknowledgment of the challenge, then confidence-building
- **Tone shift:** Move from "I understand your frustration" to "You've got this"
- **Graphics:** Consider showing insurance card visual during the preview section
- **Call-out:** Mention the interactive exercise specifically — it's a unique feature

## Key Messages to Convey

1. Insurance confusion is universal — you're not alone
2. There IS logic to insurance — it's learnable
3. This section covers both theory AND daily application
4. The interactive exercise makes learning engaging
5. Real confidence: you'll be able to help patients
