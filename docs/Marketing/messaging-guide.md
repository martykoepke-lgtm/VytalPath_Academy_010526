## Key Messages

These are the 5 core messages to weave into all marketing. Every post, listing, email, and conversation should reinforce at least one.

### 1. Affordable
> "$327 for a full year — comparable programs cost $1,000+."

Supporting points:
- 3-day full refund guarantee
- Volume pricing for teams (as low as $121/seat)
- No hidden fees, no upsells
- One price, all content included

### 2. Comprehensive
> "9 sections, 80+ lessons, 18 quizzes, and 24 procedure guides."

Supporting points:
- HIPAA, insurance & billing, medical terminology, EHR systems, patient communication, front office workflows, coding basics, referrals & prior authorization
- Not just knowledge — practical workflows you'll use on the job
- Maps to 101 knowledge statements across 7 competency domains

### 3. Hands-On
> "The only program with a built-in EHR Practice Lab."

Supporting points:
- Register patients, schedule appointments, check in/out, manage encounters
- No separate software to install or license
- Practice real workflows in a simulated environment
- Phone call simulations and mock interviews

### 4. Job-Ready
> "We don't just teach you — we help you get hired."

Supporting points:
- Resume builder
- AI-powered mock interviews
- Phone call simulator with realistic scenarios
- Insurance hotline practice
- Certificate of completion employers can verify

### 5. Flexible
> "Learn from home, on your schedule."

Supporting points:
- 100% online
- Self-paced — no class times, no commute
- Video and reading lessons you can pause and revisit
- AI study assistant available 24/7
- Full year of access

## Tone & Voice

### Who we sound like:
- A knowledgeable friend who works in healthcare and genuinely wants to help you get started
- Practical, no-nonsense, direct
- Confident but not arrogant
- Encouraging without being cheesy

### Who we DON'T sound like:
- A corporate institution ("leverage synergies in healthcare workforce readiness")
- A hype machine ("EARN $50K FROM HOME! HEALTHCARE IS BOOMING!")
- A hard-sell marketer ("LIMITED TIME! ACT NOW! ONLY 3 SPOTS LEFT!")
- An academic ("the pedagogical framework integrates competency-based assessment...")

### Voice examples:

**Good:** "Most people don't realize that 40% of a front desk job is insurance-related. That's why we spend 18 lessons on it."

**Bad:** "Our industry-leading insurance curriculum provides comprehensive coverage of payer dynamics and reimbursement methodologies."

**Good:** "You'll actually practice in a simulated EHR system — not just watch a video about one."

**Bad:** "Our cutting-edge simulation technology delivers an immersive learning experience."

**Good:** "$327 a year. That's it. No hidden fees."

**Bad:** "An unbelievable value proposition at an accessible price point."

## Competitor Positioning

When someone asks "how is this different from [X]?"

### vs. Stepful (~$1,000+)
"Stepful is a good program, but it's 3x our price and doesn't include an EHR Practice Lab. VytalPath covers the same core material for $327 with more hands-on practice tools."

### vs. Free Platforms (Alison, YouTube, Khan Academy)
"Free resources are great for overview knowledge. VytalPath goes deeper — we have 80+ structured lessons, 18 quizzes, a simulated EHR, 24 SOPs, and a certificate. It's the difference between watching a cooking show and going to culinary school."

### vs. Community College Programs ($2,000-5,000)
"Community college programs are excellent if you can commit to a set schedule and a semester timeline. VytalPath is online, self-paced, and a fraction of the cost. Some people use us as a supplement to their college program, others use us instead."

### vs. CMAA/CCMA Certification Prep
"Certification programs like CMAA focus on passing a specific exam. VytalPath focuses on building practical competency — the actual skills you use on the job. Many of our learners go on to pursue certification after completing VytalPath, and our curriculum maps to the same knowledge areas."
