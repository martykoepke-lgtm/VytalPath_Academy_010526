## VytalPath Academy — Healthcare Front Office Training

## Strategy Overview

Facebook Marketplace allows you to list services locally. Even though VytalPath is online, Marketplace listings show up in local search and get organic reach without ad spend. The key is to treat each listing like a mini landing page — clear value, low friction, direct CTA.

**Goal:** Drive signups from career changers and job seekers browsing Marketplace in your metro area.

**Cost:** $0 (organic listings only)

**Frequency:** Post 1-2 new listings per week. Rotate angles. Delete and relist stale posts every 7-10 days to stay fresh in the algorithm.

## Photo / Image Guidelines

You need 2-4 images per listing. Marketplace listings with good photos get 3-5x more views.

**What to include:**
1. **Platform screenshot** — the dashboard, a lesson page, or the EHR Practice Lab. Use a clean browser window (no bookmarks bar, no tabs).
2. **Comparison graphic** — Canva template showing VytalPath vs. competitors on price and features.
3. **Certificate preview** — screenshot of the certificate page (use a sample name).
4. **Feature highlight** — a simple graphic listing 5-6 bullet points of what's included.

**Canva tips:**
- Use 1200x1200px (square) images
- Use the VytalPath blue (#2563EB) as accent color
- Keep text large and readable on mobile
- No more than 6 lines of text on any image
