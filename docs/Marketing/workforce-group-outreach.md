## Overview

Workforce development organizations (WorkSource centers, community action agencies, WIOA programs, career colleges, and staffing agencies) are always looking for affordable, job-ready training programs to recommend to their clients. VytalPath Academy fits perfectly into their pipeline: it's affordable, self-paced, and leads directly to employable skills.

This document contains outreach templates and materials you can customize for workforce groups in your area.

---

## Target Organizations

### Tier 1: Government Workforce Programs (Highest Priority)
- **WorkSource / American Job Centers** — Federally funded one-stop career centers. Every state has them. They help job seekers find training and employment. Many can fund training through WIOA (Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act) vouchers.
- **WIOA Title I Programs** — Adult and dislocated worker programs that pay for training. If VytalPath gets on their Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL), these programs can pay the $327 directly.
- **State workforce development boards** — Set priorities for workforce funding in your region.

### Tier 2: Community Organizations
- **Community Action Agencies** — Nonprofit orgs that help low-income individuals with job training, often with funding to pay for programs.
- **Goodwill Career Centers** — Offer job training and placement services.
- **United Way / 211 referral networks** — Connect people to local resources including job training.
- **YWCA / women's career programs** — Career retraining programs specifically for women.
- **Veteran transition programs** — Military spouses and transitioning service members often look for healthcare careers.

### Tier 3: Educational Partners
- **Community colleges** — Continuing education / workforce departments. VytalPath could supplement or replace their own offerings.
- **Adult education centers / GED programs** — Students finishing their GED often want a fast path to employment.
- **Career and Technical Education (CTE) programs** — High school and post-secondary programs focused on career skills.

### Tier 4: Healthcare Industry
- **Healthcare staffing agencies** — They place temporary and permanent front office staff. Training = better candidates = more placements.
- **Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs)** — Community health centers that serve underserved populations. Often struggle with front office staffing and training.
- **Medical/dental office associations** — Local chapters of professional associations.

## Talking Points for In-Person Meetings

Use these when meeting with a workforce development coordinator, career counselor, or program director.

### Opening
"We built VytalPath Academy because there's a gap in healthcare training. Clinics can't find qualified front office staff, and most training programs are either too expensive, too shallow, or don't include any hands-on practice. We solve all three problems."

### Key Differentiators (pick 2-3 to emphasize)
1. **"We have a built-in EHR Practice Lab."** Most programs tell you about EHR systems. Ours lets you actually work in one — register patients, schedule appointments, check patients in and out. No extra software to install or license.

2. **"Our curriculum maps to 101 knowledge statements."** We track competency across 7 domains. You can see exactly what a learner knows and where they have gaps. It's not just "they finished the course" — it's "they've demonstrated competency in insurance verification, HIPAA compliance, and medical terminology."

3. **"Job readiness is built in."** Phone call simulations with realistic scenarios. AI-powered mock interviews. A resume builder. We don't just teach the knowledge — we help them get the job.

4. **"$327 is a fraction of the cost."** Comparable programs from Stepful and others run $1,000+. Community college programs are often $2,000-5,000 with a rigid schedule. We're online, self-paced, and accessible.

5. **"24 SOPs they can use on the job."** Standard operating procedures for everything from patient registration to referral management. They're not just studying for a test — they're learning workflows they'll use day one on the job.

### Handling Objections

**"Is it accredited?"**
"VytalPath Academy is a competency-based training program, not a certification exam. Our curriculum covers the same knowledge areas tested by organizations like the AAMA and NHA. The certificate of completion demonstrates that the learner has completed comprehensive training and passed assessments across all knowledge domains. Many employers in this space value demonstrated competency and practical skills over specific certifications."

**"Can your learners get WIOA funding?"**
"That's something I'd love to explore with you. We believe VytalPath qualifies for many state ETPL lists, and at $327 it's well within typical ITA (Individual Training Account) limits. If you can point me to the right contact for your state's ETPL application process, I'll get the paperwork started."

**"We already partner with a community college for healthcare training."**
"That's great — community colleges are excellent partners. VytalPath could complement what they offer. Our EHR Practice Lab and SOP library are unique features that most college programs don't include. Some organizations use us as a pre-training assessment or as supplemental content. We're also a good option for clients who can't commit to a college schedule."

**"How do I know learners actually learn something?"**
"Every learner takes 18 quizzes with an 80% passing score requirement, and we track competency across 101 individual knowledge statements in 7 domains. I can show you what the competency dashboard looks like — you'll see exactly where each learner stands."

## WIOA / ETPL Notes

If a workforce board or American Job Center expresses interest in funding learners through WIOA:

1. **Ask for the ETPL application** for your state. Every state has its own Eligible Training Provider List process.
2. **Key information they'll need:**
   - Program name: VytalPath Academy — Healthcare Front Office Training
   - Provider: Practical Informatics LLC
   - Cost: $327/year (individual)
   - Duration: Self-paced; ~10+ hours of content; typically completed in 4-8 weeks
   - Delivery: 100% online
   - Credential: Certificate of Completion
   - Occupation codes (SOC): 43-6013 (Medical Secretaries and Administrative Assistants), 29-2071 (Medical Records Specialists), 43-4171 (Receptionists and Information Clerks)
3. **Completion data:** Track completion rates once you have learners — ETPL applications often require outcome reporting.
