## The 8 Training Sections

### 1. Foundations of Healthcare

Build a foundation in healthcare administration. Understand inpatient vs. ambulatory care delivery models and the critical role of the front office.

- 3 video lessons | 1 quiz | Interactive exercise
- Estimated time: ~13 minutes

### 2. Medical Law & Compliance

Master HIPAA regulations, patient privacy rights, authorization and consent, EMTALA, healthcare fraud, and the legal framework every healthcare worker must know.

- 9 lessons (3 video + 6 reading) | 3 quizzes | Interactive PHI challenge
- Estimated time: ~90 minutes

### 3. Insurance & Billing

The most comprehensive insurance training available at this price point. From basic concepts through real-time eligibility verification, payment collection, government programs, EOBs, the revenue cycle, and financial assistance.

- 19 lessons (10 video + 9 reading) | 5 quizzes | 3 interactive explorers + term matching
- Estimated time: ~120 minutes

### 4. Front Office Workflows

Every procedure your staff needs — organized in the order they'll actually use them. From opening the office through closing, with 24 ready-to-use Standard Operating Procedures.

- 20 lessons (3 video + 17 reading) | 1 quiz | 24 SOPs included
- Estimated time: ~160 minutes + unlimited SOP reference

### 5. EHR & Practice Management

Understand how Practice Management and EHR systems work together. Covers encounter types, the encounter lifecycle, scheduling methods, phone encounters, and duplicate record management. Addresses 43% of CMAA exam content.

- 9 lessons (2 video + 7 reading) | 3 quizzes
- Estimated time: ~66 minutes

### 6. Medical Terminology

Master the language of healthcare — prefixes, roots, suffixes, abbreviations, body systems, and the Joint Commission's "Do Not Use" list. Includes interactive flashcards and a term builder workshop.

- 9 lessons (2 video + 7 reading) | 1 quiz | Flashcards + term builder
- Estimated time: ~50 minutes

### 7. EHR Practice Lab — *Key Differentiator*

A fully self-contained PM/EHR simulation built directly into the platform. No external system required. Students practice real workflows — registering patients, scheduling appointments, checking patients in and out, managing encounters, and routing messages — in a realistic clinical environment.

- Provider schedule, appointments, patient charts, message inbox
- 14 encounter types supported (NP, EST, AWV, TCM, Preventive, Procedure & more)
- 48-hour timed sessions with reset capability
- Unlimited practice

### 8. Job Readiness

Career preparation tools that go beyond the textbook. Students practice real-world scenarios before they ever step into a clinic.

- **Phone Call Simulator** — AI-powered patient call practice with performance feedback
- **Mock Interview** — Realistic healthcare front office interview preparation
- **Resume Builder** — Step-by-step resume creation with healthcare-specific guidance
- **Insurance Hotline Practice** — Simulated insurance company calls for eligibility verification
