# **VIDEO: FOUNDATIONS OF HEALTHCARE — SECTION INTRO**

**Video Type:** Section Introduction (Coach Mode)
**Duration:** 2–3 minutes
**Tone:** Warm, grounding, welcoming
**Placement:** Top of Foundations section page, before Module 1

## **SCENE 1: Welcome**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[warm\] Welcome to your very first section — Foundations of Healthcare. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] Before you can schedule appointments, verify insurance, or check patients in and out \[pause\] — you need to understand \[emphasis\] where that work happens \[pause\] and \[emphasis\] who you'll be working with. \[pause\]

\[warm\] That's what this section is all about. \[pause\] We're going to build the foundation that everything else in this program sits on top of. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Delivery: Warm and grounding. This is the student's first real lesson moment.
* Tone: Welcoming. Make them feel like they're in the right place.

---

## **SCENE 2: Where Healthcare Happens**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] Healthcare doesn't all happen in one place. \[pause\] And understanding the \[emphasis\] different care settings \[pause\] is the first thing you need to know. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] When most people think of healthcare, they think of hospitals. \[pause\] But here's the reality — \[pause\] over eighty percent of healthcare happens \[emphasis\] outside the hospital. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] It happens in primary care clinics. \[pause\] Urgent care centers. \[pause\] Specialty offices — cardiology, orthopedics, dermatology. \[pause\] Imaging centers. \[pause\] Outpatient surgery centers. \[pause\] Physical therapy offices. \[pause\] Even telehealth visits from the patient's home. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] These are all \[emphasis\] ambulatory care settings — \[pause\] places where the patient comes in, gets care, and goes home the same day. \[pause\] And this is where the vast majority of front office jobs are. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] But hospitals matter too. \[pause\] You'll receive patients who were just discharged. \[pause\] You'll coordinate follow-up care after a hospital stay. \[pause\] You'll handle records that come from inpatient episodes. \[pause\] So you need to understand both worlds. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Delivery: Building a mental picture. Paint the landscape of care settings.
* Tone: Informational but engaging. This should feel like a tour guide opening a map.

---

## **SCENE 3: What You'll Learn**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[warm\] In this section, you'll start with two modules. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] In the first module — Healthcare Delivery — \[pause\] you'll learn the two main care delivery models. \[pause\] How inpatient care works — where the patient stays in the hospital and everything comes to them. \[pause\] And how ambulatory care works — where the patient moves between different appointments, locations, and providers over weeks or months. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] You'll see why this difference matters \[pause\] — because the same administrative tasks look very different depending on which model you're working in. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] In the second module — Your Role in Healthcare — \[pause\] you'll learn about the \[emphasis\] full healthcare team. \[pause\] The clinical side — physicians, nurse practitioners, nurses, medical assistants. \[pause\] And the administrative side — front desk, medical administrative assistants, billing specialists, and office managers. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] You'll learn exactly \[emphasis\] where you fit in \[pause\] — what you're responsible for, what's outside your scope, \[pause\] and how the front office and clinical team connect throughout the day. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] Then you'll follow a patient \[emphasis\] through the entire journey — \[pause\] from the moment they first call your office, \[pause\] through check-in, the visit, and check-out. \[pause\] You'll also learn how to handle patients with special needs — \[pause\] language barriers, visual impairments, mobility challenges, \[pause\] and patients who need help with technology like self-service kiosks. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Delivery: Clear and structured. Each module gets its own beat.
* Tone: Like a coach previewing the game plan. Organized and purposeful.

## **SCENE 4: Why This Matters**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[encouraging\] Here's the thing. \[pause\] A lot of training programs skip this part. \[pause\] They jump straight into tasks — here's how to check someone in, here's how to verify insurance. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] But when you understand \[emphasis\] the bigger picture first — \[pause\] how the system works, where your role fits, what the patient is going through — \[pause\] everything else you learn will make more sense. \[pause\] The tasks stop being just steps on a checklist \[pause\] and start being part of something you actually understand. \[pause\]

\[warm\] This foundation will carry you through every section that follows — \[pause\] compliance, insurance, workflows, EHR systems, and beyond. \[pause\]

\[encouraging\] So let's get started. \[pause\] You're in the right place, \[pause\] and this is going to be a great start to your training. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Delivery: Motivating close. Connect this section to everything that comes after.
* Tone: Encouraging without being over the top. Genuine and practical.
* Final line should feel like a confident send-off into Module 1.
