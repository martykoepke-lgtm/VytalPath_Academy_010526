# **VIDEO 2: Key Insurance Terms**

**Duration:** \~5 minutes **Learning Objective:** Understand and explain the essential insurance terms used daily in front office operations

---

## **SCENE 1: Opening (0:00–0:25)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[warm\] Let's cover the terms you'll use every day. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] These are the words that get thrown around in every clinic \[pause\] and once you understand what they mean \[pause\] conversations with patients and insurance companies get a lot easier. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Key Insurance Terms"  
* Visual: Title slide, transition to avatar

---

## **SCENE 2: Premium and Deductible (0:25–1:30)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] A \[emphasis\] premium \[pause\] is the monthly payment to have insurance coverage. \[pause\] The patient pays this whether they use healthcare services or not. \[pause\] Think of it like a membership fee. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] A \[emphasis\] deductible \[pause\] is the amount the patient must pay out of pocket before insurance starts covering services. \[pause\]

\[warm\] Here's an example. \[pause\] If someone has a one thousand dollar deductible \[pause\] they pay the first one thousand dollars of their healthcare costs each year. \[pause\] After that \[pause\] insurance kicks in. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Premium" then "Deductible"  
* Visual: Simple graphics showing money flow  
* Key point: Deductible resets annually

---

## **SCENE 3: Copay and Coinsurance (1:30–2:45)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] A \[emphasis\] copay \[pause\] is a fixed amount the patient pays at the time of service. \[pause\] Twenty dollars for a primary care visit \[pause\] fifty dollars for a specialist \[pause\] it depends on the plan. \[pause\]

\[warm\] This is probably the term you'll use most often. \[pause\] Patients will ask you about their copay all the time. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] \[emphasis\] Coinsurance \[pause\] is a percentage the patient pays after meeting their deductible. \[pause\] If the plan has twenty percent coinsurance \[pause\] the patient pays twenty percent of the cost \[pause\] and insurance pays eighty percent. \[pause\]

\[warm\] The key difference \[pause\] copay is a flat amount \[pause\] coinsurance is a percentage. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Copay" then "Coinsurance"  
* Visual: Side-by-side comparison — flat fee vs percentage  
* Key point: Copay \= flat amount, Coinsurance \= percentage

---

## **SCENE 4: Out-of-Pocket Maximum (2:45–3:30)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] The \[emphasis\] out-of-pocket maximum \[pause\] is the most a patient will pay in a year. \[pause\]

\[warm\] This is actually good news for patients. \[pause\] Once they hit this amount \[pause\] insurance covers one hundred percent of covered services for the rest of the year. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] It's like a safety net. \[pause\] No matter how much care they need \[pause\] there's a ceiling on what they'll pay. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Out-of-Pocket Maximum"  
* Visual: Graphic showing ceiling/cap concept  
* Key point: Protection for patients with high healthcare costs

---

## **SCENE 5: Network Status (3:30–4:15)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] \[emphasis\] In-network \[pause\] means the provider has a contract with the insurance company. \[pause\] Patients usually pay less for in-network care. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] \[emphasis\] Out-of-network \[pause\] means no contract exists. \[pause\] Patients pay more \[pause\] and some plans don't cover out-of-network care at all. \[pause\]

\[warm\] This comes up a lot. \[pause\] Patients will call and ask \[pause\] do you take my insurance? \[pause\] What they're really asking is \[pause\] are you in-network with my plan? \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "In-Network vs Out-of-Network"  
* Visual: Diagram showing contracted vs non-contracted providers  
* Key point: "Do you take my insurance?" \= "Are you in-network?"

---

## **SCENE 6: Prior Authorization (4:15–4:45)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] \[emphasis\] Prior authorization \[pause\] means insurance requires approval before certain services. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] Without authorization \[pause\] the service might not be covered \[pause\] even if it's medically necessary. \[pause\]

\[warm\] This is one you'll hear about from both patients and clinical staff. \[pause\] We'll cover the practical side of handling authorizations later in the operations module. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Prior Authorization"  
* Visual: Approval workflow icon  
* Key point: Required BEFORE the service, not after

\[warm\] You don't need to memorize definitions. \[pause\] You need to understand the concepts \[pause\] so you can help real patients with real questions. \[pause\]

# **VIDEO 3: Insurance Operations — Eligibility & Payments**

**Duration:** \~5 minutes **Learning Objective:** Learn to verify insurance eligibility and collect patient payments confidently

---

## **SCENE 1: Opening (0:00–0:25)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[warm\] Now let's get practical. \[pause\] You know the terms \[pause\] you know the payers. \[pause\] Let's talk about what you'll actually do with that knowledge every day. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Insurance Operations: Eligibility & Payments"  
* Visual: Title slide, transition to avatar

---

## **SCENE 2: Why Verify (0:25–1:00)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] Eligibility verification confirms that a patient's insurance is active \[pause\] and covers the services they're scheduled to receive. \[pause\]

\[warm\] Think of it as checking the locks before you open the door. \[pause\] You want to make sure everything is in order \[emphasis\] before the patient arrives. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Eligibility Verification"  
* Visual: Checklist icon or verification workflow

---

## **SCENE 3: When and How to Verify (1:00–2:00)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] Always verify eligibility before the appointment \[pause\] ideally 48 to 72 hours ahead. \[pause\] This gives you time to resolve any issues. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] Check that the insurance plan is active. \[pause\] Check that the patient is covered under the plan. \[pause\] Check that your clinic and provider are in-network. \[pause\] Check for any authorization requirements. \[pause\]

\[warm\] If there's a problem \[pause\] the plan terminated \[pause\] the patient isn't listed \[pause\] or authorization is needed \[pause\] you can address it before the patient arrives. \[pause\] \[emphasis\] That's much better than discovering issues at check-in. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Verify 48–72 Hours Ahead"  
* Visual: Checklist with four verification items  
* Key point: Prevention is always better than check-in surprises

---

## **SCENE 4: Documentation (2:00–2:45)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[instructional\] Document what you verified and when. \[pause\] If you spoke to someone at the insurance company \[pause\] note their name and reference number. \[pause\]

\[warm\] This protects you \[pause\] it protects the clinic \[pause\] and it creates a paper trail if there's ever a dispute. \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] And here's something important. \[pause\] Insurance changes constantly. \[pause\] People change jobs \[pause\] switch plans \[pause\] add or remove dependents. \[pause\] \[emphasis\] Never assume last month's information is still accurate. \[pause\] Verify every time. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Document Everything"  
* Visual: Documentation screen or notes example  
* Key point: Insurance changes — verify every visit

---

## **SCENE 5: Collecting Payments (2:45–3:45)**

### **AVATAR SCRIPT — Copy directly into HeyGen**

\[warm\] Collecting payments can feel awkward \[pause\] but it's a normal part of healthcare. \[pause\] Patients expect to pay something \[pause\] they just want to know what to expect. \[pause\]

\[instructional\] Know the copay before the patient arrives. \[pause\] When they check in \[pause\] let them know the amount clearly and confidently. \[pause\]

\[warm\] You might say something like \[pause\] your copay today is thirty dollars. \[pause\] We accept cash \[pause\] check \[pause\] or card. \[pause\] Would you like to take care of that now? \[pause\]

\[matter-of-fact\] Keep it simple. \[pause\] Keep it friendly. \[pause\] Don't apologize for the amount \[pause\] it's not your policy \[pause\] it's their plan. \[pause\]

### **PRODUCTION NOTES**

* Display text: "Collecting Patient Payments"  
* Visual: Check-in counter scenario  
* Key point: Confident, friendly, straightforward
